C2Society

A Las Vegas nonprofit dedicated to spreading security awareness through the local community.

C2Society started in 2020 as DC702, the city's DEF CON group, and grew into a nonprofit that brings together people from every corner of security and IT, from seasoned hackers to people who have never held a pick. The goal is simple: get hands on the thing. Everything we build is meant to be touched, broken, and taken apart, because that's how people actually learn how systems fail.

  • DC702 meetups

    Weekly sessions for lockpicking, Hack The Box, and whatever members bring in. Open to newcomers, and home to the local TOOOL chapter.

  • The Petting Zoo

    A workshop-style CTF that introduces people to capture the flag. Run at DEF CON 30 and 31 and for government, military, and law enforcement groups.

  • DEF CON

    Black badge contests, including the Cube and Crack the Core, plus workshops and volunteer time across the villages.

  • Building CTF challenges

    Notes and tooling for writing CTF challenges, plus our CTFd setup for per-team containers.

Find C2Society at c2society.org and github.com/c2society.
