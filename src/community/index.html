Talks & community

Conference talks, contests, workshops, and volunteer work, mostly through C2Society and DC702.

Talks

  • The Adversarial Mindset

    Las Vegas ISSA, Q4 2023

    What an adversarial mindset is, how to apply it in professional life, what it means across different kinds of engagements, and how our biases get in the way of thinking like an attacker. A fresh angle on a topic a lot of organizations are trying to teach.

  • Full Stack for Hackers

    SAINTCON, 2023

    How hackers can borrow the skill set of full-stack developers: the layers attackers touch, the technologies worth knowing, and where a hacker's needs differ from a traditional developer's. It began as a workshop for the Air Force and grew into a conference talk.

  • The Song Must Go On

    Shmoocon, 2023

    Attacking smart jukeboxes with a Flipper Zero. The talk walked through exploiting the jukebox, how it could be turned against people, and why our industry needs to think about the features we ship now that hacking tools ("toys") are so easy to get.

DEF CON

Through DC702 and C2Society I've contributed to DEF CON through contests, workshops, and volunteering. We've hosted multiple black badge contests, including the Cube and Crack the Core.

I've also hosted and been lead instructor for two CTF-style DEF CON workshops (The Petting Zoo, at DEF CON 30 and 31) that help people get into capture the flag events, and volunteered with the Social Engineering Village, the Red Team Village, and the Lockpicking Village through TOOOL.

Elsewhere

Over the years I've spoken in a lot of settings: an interview on blockchain for theCUBE at HoshoCon 2018; workshops for the Air Force, FBI, Department of Energy, and local police; company security trainings; presentations at local colleges; and talks in DEF CON villages such as the Lockpicking Village.
